# Request for proposals: national SME e-invoicing and digitalisation policy

**Issuing entity**: Ministry of Finance, Republic of Meridia (fictional). **Reference**: MoF/RFP/2026/017. **Date**: 20 October 2026.

## 1. Background

Meridia is a lower-middle-income country of about 41 million people. Micro, small and medium enterprises account for roughly 93 per cent of registered firms and an estimated 55 per cent of private employment, yet contribute a small share of value-added tax receipts. A mandatory electronic invoicing system was introduced for large taxpayers in 2023 and extended to medium firms in 2025. Adoption among small and micro firms remains low; the Tax Authority estimates that under one in five issue electronic invoices. The Government's Digital Economy Strategy 2030 sets a target of universal electronic invoicing by 2030.

## 2. Objectives of the assignment

The Ministry seeks an advisory partner to:

1. Diagnose the barriers to electronic invoicing adoption among small and micro enterprises, including cost, capability, connectivity, trust and incentives.
2. Benchmark the experience of at least four comparable countries that extended e-invoicing to small firms.
3. Develop and appraise policy options, including phasing, thresholds, incentives, simplified regimes, free or subsidised tooling, and enforcement design, with their fiscal, economic and distributional impacts.
4. Recommend a policy package with an implementation roadmap, institutional arrangements, a monitoring framework and a communication plan for firms, intermediaries and the public.
5. Prepare materials for the Minister of Finance and, if required, for the Cabinet Economic Committee.

## 3. Scope

In scope: small and micro enterprises in all sectors; the interaction with VAT registration thresholds and the simplified tax regime; the roles of the Tax Authority, the Ministry of Trade and Industry, the SME Development Agency, the Central Bank (payments), banks and payment service providers, accounting software vendors, chambers of commerce and merchant associations. Out of scope: large-taxpayer systems already in place; customs.

## 4. Deliverables

- D1: Inception report with approach, methodology, stakeholder map and workplan (week 2).
- D2: Diagnostic report with international benchmarks (week 8).
- D3: Policy options paper with impact assessment (week 14).
- D4: Final policy recommendation, implementation roadmap, monitoring framework and communication plan, with an executive summary for the Minister and a Cabinet-level brief (week 20).
- Presentations to the Ministry steering committee at each stage.

## 5. Governance and timeline

The Ministry will appoint a project manager (Director, Tax Policy Unit) and a steering committee chaired by the Deputy Minister for Fiscal Policy. The Minister of Finance is the ultimate decision maker; Cabinet approval may be sought for legislative changes. The assignment runs for 20 weeks from contract signature.

## 6. Evaluation criteria

Understanding of the problem and context (25), methodology and workplan (25), relevant experience (20), quality of proposed team (15), value for money (15).

## 7. Constraints and considerations

The 2027 budget is tabled in March 2027; any fiscal incentive must be costed for it. The Government has stated publicly that it will not raise the VAT registration threshold. Local elections are expected in the second half of 2027. Deliverables are to be provided in English; an Arabic executive summary may be requested.
