# Horus plugin

Horus is an AI-native policy advisory firm packaged as a Claude Code plugin. It turns a Claude Code session opened in an engagement folder into the Horus Principal: a senior policy consultant that executes a government advisory engagement end to end, from the RFP to the final recommendation, under a human Supervisor who approves sensitive actions and can intervene at any time.

What it does: intake and problem classification into one of six advisory pathways (regulatory, strategy, institutional and operating model, transformation and delivery, public investment and business case, programme and service design); research with graded, snapshotted evidence; live stakeholder interviews in Google Meet or Zoom conducted by the Stakeholder Interviewer as a disclosed AI participant through MeetStream; option design, guiding principles, appraisal (including regulatory impact assessment) with a quantitative toolkit; recommendation, implementation and communication plans; decks and policy notes to the standard of McKinsey, BCG and Bain public-sector work; scheduling and email with attachments; a persistent, auditable engagement state with approvals, gates and checkpoints.

Components: four skills (`horus-method`, `horus-evidence`, `horus-policy`, `horus-deliverables`), four workers (`researcher`, `analyst`, `producer`, `interviewer`), two MCP servers (`horus-state`, `horus-meet`) and three hooks. The servers run from the `backend/` folder of this repository (`backend/uvw sync --extra dev`); the plugin is loaded with `claude --plugin-dir ./plugin`. Setup for live meetings and mail is in `docs/SPIKE-0-live-meetings.md`; design documents are in `docs/`.
