# Phase 1: Mobilise

Convert a confirmed brief and pathway into the way of working: framing and hypotheses, the approach and workplan, the stakeholder map with interview requests, the first politics analyses, and a kickoff deck. Phase 1 ends at AP1, the kickoff, presented by the Supervisor with Horus attending.

**When Horus uses this:** `engagement.phase` is `P1` and gate `AP1` is not `passed`.

## Purpose and exit criteria

Agree with the client how the engagement will run before any finding is produced, so later readouts are judged against a plan the client approved. Gate AP1 (STATE_SCHEMA.md section 7) reports `ready` when: `brief.status` is `confirmed`; `approach` and `workplan` are at version 1 or later; `approach.frameworks` come from the pathway pack; every workstream has at least one `hypotheses` record; every stakeholder at `engagement.priority: 1` has a `client_request_meeting` action; `profiles` exist for the ultimate decision maker and the client entity; the kickoff deck version has all QA audits passed and its release approval; the kickoff `meetings` record lists `decisions_sought`; and decisions of kind `methodology` and `workplan` are `confirmed`. Rule 19 adds `engagement.pathway.primary` set with the `pathway` decision `confirmed`. Exit is `set_gate AP1 passed` on the gate decision made in the kickoff, then `set_phase P2`.

## Entry checks

Run `status` and `view gate_checklist`. Required: `gates.AP0.status: passed`; `brief.status: confirmed` at version 1 or later with `client_roles` filled; `engagement.pathway` with `primary`, `classified_in` and `confirmed_in`; no inbound `communications` left at `received`; a kickoff `meetings` record at `requested` or `scheduled`; the two P0 `profiles` started. If any is missing, finish Phase 0; do not patch around it.

## Step by step

1. **Load the pack.** Read `horus-policy/references/pathways/<id>.md` for the primary and secondary pathways: `Diagnostic lens`, `Frameworks`, `Stakeholder emphasis`, `Core Tier-2 deliverables and structures`, `Gate criteria variants`. Read the framework files it names under `references/frameworks/` and decide which you will actually use.
2. **Frame the problem.** Build the issue tree from the reframed problem statement through the diagnostic lens. `create` `analyses` `kind: problem_framing` (`frameworks`, `inputs → DOC and EVD`, `method_notes`, `content` holding the tree, `findings`, `limitations`, `narrative_path`, `status: reviewed`). `create` one `hypotheses` record per leaf worth testing: `statement` (falsifiable), `kind` (`diagnostic`, `causal`, `political`, `stakeholder`, `implementation`), `parent`, `status: untested`, `confidence`, `implications`. At least one per workstream; eight to fifteen in total.
3. **Write the approach v1.** `create` the `approach` singleton, `status: proposed`: `problem_framing → ANA`, `methodology_narrative` (the deck paragraph), `frameworks[] {id, name, source_org, rationale, used_in_phases}`, `workstreams[] {id: WS-n, name, objective, key_questions, outputs}`, `analytical_plan.research_questions[] {id: RQ-nn, question, hypotheses → HYP, method, data_sources, workstream, status: planned}`, `engagement_plan_summary {interview_targets → STK, workshops, cadence}`, `quality_plan {grade_floor, review_policy, traceability_required: true}`, `governance {steering_members, pm_cadence, decision_forums}`. An RQ that tests no hypothesis is curiosity.
4. **Write the workplan v1.** `create` the `workplan` singleton, `status: proposed`: `phases[] {id, name, start, end, gate}`, `activities[] {id: A-nn, phase, workstream, name, owner, start, end, depends_on, outputs}`, `milestones[] {id: M-nn, name, date, kind, linked}`, `gates[] {id, name, criteria, decisions_required}` from the pack's gate variants, `cadence {pm_checkin, steering, status_note}`. If the brief's timeline cannot be met, say so in the deck rather than compressing P2.
5. **Map the stakeholders.** Using `Stakeholder emphasis`, `create` or `update` `stakeholders` for every actor who can decide, block, implement or be hurt: `category`, `decision_role`, `influence`, `interest`, `stance: unknown` unless there is `stance_basis`, `relationships`, `engagement {priority 1 to 3, purpose, format: interview, owner, status: not_planned}`, `last_reviewed`; check with `view stakeholder_matrix`. For every priority 1, `create` an `actions` record `kind: client_request_meeting` (`detail` naming purpose and hypotheses, `owner → STK (PM)`, `priority: P1`, `linked: AP1`) asking the PM for an introduction, and a `meetings` record `kind: interview`, `status: proposed`, `conduct_mode: interviewer`, `participants`, `purpose`, `hypotheses_to_test`. Say on the action that Horus joins as a disclosed AI participant, schedules by email once introduced (decision 17), and on Google Meet must be admitted by the host.
6. **Finish the profiles and start the lens.** Up to four `researcher` runs (the P1 budget): complete the decision-maker and client-entity profiles; profile the sponsor and the most influential non-client actor. Brief: subject, open questions in the profile, public record only, inferences labelled, outputs `sources`, `evidence`, memo. `update profiles` with `career`, `priorities_stated`, `track_record`, `positions_on_topic`, `political_calendar`, `implications_for_engagement`, `review {personal_data_minimised, defamation_check, reviewed_by, date}`. `create` `analyses` `kind: decision_maker_lens` (`subject`, `priorities`, `red_lines`, `decision_style`, `calendar`, `framing_guidance`, `profile → PRF`) at `draft`; P2 completes it.
7. **Raise the data requests.** `create` `actions` `kind: client_request_data` or `client_request_document` for each dataset, register, budget or internal report an RQ needs: `detail` (fields, period, format), `owner → STK (PM)`, `due`, `linked: RQ ids`.
8. **Set up the kickoff.** `update` the kickoff `meetings` record: `conduct_mode: principal_co_led`, `participants` (PM, sponsor, steering, Supervisor, Horus) with `speaker_label` placeholders, `purpose`, `objectives`, `decisions_sought[]` (approve the methodology; approve the workplan and cadence; confirm the interview list and introductions; confirm the data requests; confirm governance), `agenda[] {item, minutes, lead}`, `hypotheses_to_test`, `open_questions_to_close`, `plan {disclosure_script_id, consent_terms, attribution_rule}`. `create` `decisions` `kind: methodology` and `kind: workplan`, `status: proposed`, `proposed_by: horus`, `forum → MTG`.
9. **Produce the kickoff deck.** `create` `deliverables` `type: kickoff_deck`, `tier: T1`, `audience → STK`, `purpose`, `for_meeting → MTG`, `storyboard_path`, `template`, `status: in_progress`. Write the ghost deck (action title, exhibit, body, evidence ids, so-what per slide). Delegate to `producer`: deliverable id, tier, storyboard, evidence ids, template, QA level full; it renders, runs the five audits and calls `register_version`. A fresh `producer` reviews (file and rubric only); fix blockers and majors; register the next version. `request_approval` on the version, action class `tier0_tier1_release`, risk `medium`; `release_version` marks it `supervisor_approved`, and `sent` follows when the carrying communication is logged in `sent_in`. `update meetings.prep_pack → DEL`.
10. **Send the pre-read.** `create` outbound `communications` to the PM with deck and agenda, `related → MTG and DEL`; approval class `client_pm_email`, risk `medium`; send; `update meetings.pre_read_sent → COM`.
11. **Hold the kickoff** per `alignment-point-protocol.md`, co-led case; capture, request the gate transition, `checkpoint`.

## Pathway hooks

`Diagnostic lens` shapes the issue tree: regulatory briefs decompose by market failure, compliance landscape and instrument fit; strategy by position, trends, capabilities and choices; institutional by mandate, functions, decision rights and incentives; delivery along the delivery chain; investment by strategic case, demand and affordability; service by user journey, barriers and cost to serve. `Frameworks` names the catalogue ids for `approach.frameworks`. `Gate criteria variants` adds pack lines to `workplan.gates` (the legal baseline RQ at AP2 on the regulatory pathway; the affordability envelope at AP1 on the investment pathway). `Stakeholder emphasis` sets who is priority 1: regulators and regulated firms; line ministries and delivery agencies; the finance ministry; users and front-line staff.

## Client-facing artefact

Kickoff deck, Tier 1, stamped `DRAFT FOR DISCUSSION` on release. Section order: (1) executive summary: what we heard, how we will work, what we need; (2) context and our understanding of the problem, reframed; (3) objectives and scope, in and out; (4) pathway and methodology: lens, frameworks, appraisal method, core deliverables; (5) workplan and governance: phases, gates, cadence, steering and decision forums; (6) stakeholder map and the interviews requested (power and interest grid, no stance where unknown); (7) hypotheses we will test; (8) data and document requests with dates; (9) risks to the engagement and their management; (10) decisions sought today; (11) next steps and the first two weeks. Twelve to fifteen slides. Nothing from `profiles` appears; the lens becomes framing choices, not a slide.

## Capture after the alignment point

From the transcript or the Supervisor's notes: `update decisions` `methodology` and `workplan` to `confirmed` with `made_by → STK[]`, `forum → MTG`, `date`, `rationale`; `update approach` and `workplan` to `approved` with `approved_in → DEC`. `create` `client_inputs` for every comment on the deck (`kind: feedback`, `preference`, `directive`, `instruction`; `about {target → DEL version, section}`; `disposition` with rationale); a directive on who may be interviewed also changes `stakeholders.engagement`. `create` `open_questions` with owners and `blocking`. `create` `actions`: introductions promised (`stakeholder_outreach`), data dates, documents to be sent. `create` `evidence` for sponsor statements of priority (`kind: position`, `attribution` per the meeting rule, `about → STK`) and set `stakeholders.stance` with `stance_basis`. Where feedback changes the plan, `append` to `workplan.change_log` and register the next deck version with `based_on_feedback`. `create` the `gate` decision (`gate: AP1`, `forum → MTG`, `status: confirmed`); `request_approval`, action class `gate_transition`, risk `high`; `set_gate AP1 passed`; `set_phase P2`. Minutes email under `client_pm_email`.

## Pitfalls

- Hypotheses that cannot be wrong ("stakeholder alignment is important"). If no evidence could refute it, it is a heading.
- Workstreams organised by deliverable rather than by question. The lens gives the decomposition; deliverables come in P5.
- Interview requests without a one-sentence purpose. The PM will ask why.
- Priority-1 lists of twenty names. Eight to twelve is what P2 can hold within the 70 per cent threshold; demote the rest.
- A kickoff deck that presents findings. There are none yet; profile-derived insight shown as findings exposes the `sensitive-personal` layer.
- Forgetting that the host must admit Horus on Google Meet. Write it into the request now, not on the day.
- Skipping the fresh-instance review because it is "only a kickoff". The first deck sets the bar the client applies to every later one.

## Checkpoint template

- `done`: framing (ANA id) with N hypotheses; approach v1 and workplan v1 (status); N priority-1 requests (ACT ids); profiles (PRF ids); lens draft (ANA id); kickoff deck (DEL id, version, QA status); pre-read sent (COM id).
- `decided`: methodology and workplan (DEC ids, status); interview list changes and directives from the kickoff (CIN ids).
- `learned`: what the sponsor cares about that the brief did not say; stakeholders the client is reluctant to let us meet; data the client does not have.
- `next`: gate transition approval (APV id); scheduling emails; research fan-out briefs (RQ ids); first weekly status note date.
- `blockers`: unconfirmed decisions; missing introductions; data requests without an owner date.
