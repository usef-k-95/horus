# Phase 0: Intake and classification

Turn an RFP or terms of reference into a confirmed brief, a classified problem with a selected pathway, and an agreed way to start. Phase 0 ends at AP0, the one alignment point closed by email rather than by a meeting.

**When Horus uses this:** `status` reports no engagement in the folder, or `engagement.phase` is `P0` and gate `AP0` is not `passed`.

## Purpose and exit criteria

Understand what is being asked, decide how Horus will work on it, and get the client to confirm both before any analysis starts. The gate is AP0 in STATE_SCHEMA.md section 7. The server reports `ready` when: the `brief` has objectives, scope in and out, required deliverables mapped and a timeline; a `problem_classification` analysis exists; `engagement.pathway.primary` is set; the client PM, sponsor and ultimate decision maker exist as `stakeholders`; every P1 clarification is `answered` or `parked`; and decisions of kind `scope` and `pathway` are `confirmed`. Exit is `set_gate AP0 passed` on a confirmed `gate` decision whose `channel` is the PM's reply, then `set_phase P1`.

## Entry checks

- The working folder is the engagement folder (decision 5 in docs/DECISIONS.md); if `status` reports no `.horus/`, step 1 initialises it.
- The RFP, terms of reference or invitation exists as a file, pasted text or email, with attachments.
- The Supervisor is named, so `engagement.team.supervisors` and `settings.autonomy.profile` can be set. Phase 0 is the only phase that starts from an empty state.

## Step by step

1. **Initialise the engagement.** `create` the `engagement` singleton: `code`, `name`, `client {entity, acronym, country, level, sector}`, `policy_domain`, `languages`, `currency`, `price_base_year`, `fiscal_year_start`, `phase: P0`, `dates.received`, `team.supervisors`, `settings.autonomy {profile: default}`, `settings.meetings {disclosure_jurisdiction from client.country, recording_retention: delete_after_verification, off_record_handling: remove}`, `settings.evidence {grade_floor: B, two_source_rule: true}`, `settings.research_budget` (default P0: two runs, 40 fetches each). Unknown values go in `notes` as provisional, never invented.
2. **Bank the documents.** `import_document` every file into `inbox/`, which creates `documents` with `kind` (`rfp`, `terms_of_reference`, `contract`, `client_report`), `origin: client`, `received_on`, `path`, `hash`, `processing.status: received`. If the material came by email, `create` the inbound `communications` record (`channel: email`, `direction: inbound`, `from`, `classification: client_pm`, `body_path`, `attachments → DOC[]`, `status: received`).
3. **Read and mine.** Read each document in full; `update documents` with `summary` and `processing.status: summarised`. `create` one `sources` record per client document (`type: client_document`, `tier: A` for what the client asked, `tier_rationale`, `independence_group: client`, `interest_declared: government`). Extract mandate facts as `evidence` (`kind: fact` or `statistic`, `sources`, `corroboration: single_source`, `confidence`), linked through `documents.key_points`. A figure the RFP asserts about the world stays single-source until P2 corroborates it.
4. **Draft the brief v0.** `create` the `brief` singleton, `status: draft`, `version: 0`: `rfp_documents`, `issued_by`, `received_on`, `title`, `context_summary`, `problem_statement {as_given, as_reframed, reframing_rationale}`, `objectives[] {id: OBJ-n, statement, measurable, indicator}`, `scope_in`, `scope_out`, `required_deliverables[] {id, description, format, due}`, `timeline {start, end, milestones}`, `constraints[] {kind, statement}`, `evaluation_criteria`, `client_roles`, `assumptions`, `success_criteria`. The reframing is where judgment goes: what the client is really deciding, in one sentence, and why that differs from the text.
5. **List clarifications.** Each gap becomes an `open_questions` record: `kind: clarification`, `raised_by: principal`, `raised_in: <session id>`, `owner → STK (PM)`, `priority`, `blocking: AP0` for anything that changes scope, deliverables or the decision maker. Five to eight questions that matter; the rest `parked` with a note.
6. **Create the initial stakeholders.** `create` `stakeholders` for the client organisation (`kind: organisation`, `is_client: true`), the PM (`client_role: pm`, `category: client_pm`), the sponsor (`client_role: sponsor`, `category: client_decision_maker`), the ultimate decision maker (`client_role: ultimate_decision_maker`, `decision_role: decide`) and named steering members. Set `decision_role` by RAPID, `influence` and `interest` null with a note where unknown, `stance: unknown`, `engagement {priority, purpose, format, status: not_planned}`, `last_reviewed`. `update brief.client_roles` with the ids.
7. **Classify the problem.** Work through `references/classification.md`: is a regulatory instrument at stake, a funding decision, an underperforming programme, an institution to redesign, a direction to set, a service to design. `create` `analyses` `kind: problem_classification` with `title`, `question`, `inputs → DOC and EVD`, `method_notes`, `content {signals[] {question, answer, evidence}, candidates[] {pathway, fit 1 to 5, rationale}, primary, secondary[], hybrid_notes, revisit_triggers[]}`, `findings`, `limitations`, `status: reviewed`; pathway ids are the enumeration in STATE_SCHEMA.md section 4. `update engagement.pathway {primary, secondary, rationale, classified_in → ANA}`. `create` two `decisions`, `status: proposed`, `proposed_by: horus`: `kind: pathway` (primary and secondary named) and `kind: scope` (the reframed problem, objectives and scope). Confirm the fit against the chosen pack's `Classification signals` before moving on.
8. **Start the two profiles.** Delegate two `researcher` instances in parallel, the whole P0 budget. Brief A, decision-maker lens: the subject STK; stated priorities, red lines, decision style, political calendar, positions on the topic; public record only; inferences labelled; outputs `sources`, `evidence` and a memo; credibility floor B; 40 fetches. Brief B, client entity: structure, leadership, mandate, recent reforms, budget if public; same outputs. The Principal then `create`s the `profiles` records (`subject`, `scope`, `summary`, `implications_for_engagement`, `overall_confidence`, `review`, `sources`; confidentiality fixed to `sensitive-personal`) and sets `stakeholders.profile`. Nothing from a profile is ever pasted into a client artefact.
9. **Draft the AP0 email.** Write it as Markdown and `create` an outbound `communications` record: `channel: email`, `direction: outbound`, `to → STK (PM)`, `cc` the sponsor only if the PM asked, `classification: client_pm` (`client_dm` if the sponsor is addressed), `subject`, `summary`, `body_path`, `related → DOC`, `status: draft`. `create` an `actions` record `kind: client_request_meeting` for the kickoff, `owner → STK (PM)`, `linked: AP1`. Approval: `request_approval` on the communication, action class `client_pm_email`, risk `medium` (`decision_maker_email`, risk `high`, with the sponsor on the To line); the Supervisor decides with `decide_approval`; under shadow the result is `not_required`. Send through the mail path (Gmail connector, or `horus-mail` when there is an attachment); the outbound guard checks the approval. `update communications {status: sent, external_ref}`.
10. **Checkpoint.** Run `validate`, then `checkpoint` with the template below.

## Pathway hooks

Classification is this phase's pathway hook. For each candidate, read `Classification signals` in `horus-policy/references/pathways/<id>.md` and score fit 1 to 5 with the signal that drove the score. Hybrids: a strategy that must end in a law or licensing regime is `strategy` primary, `regulatory` secondary; a funding request that presupposes a new agency is `public_investment_business_case` primary, `institutional_operating_model` secondary; a programme that "is not working" is `transformation_delivery` unless the ask is to redesign it from the user up, which is `programme_service_design`. Record hybrids in `content.hybrid_notes` and `engagement.pathway.secondary`, and set `revisit_triggers` so P2 knows which finding would change the classification.

## Client-facing artefact

The AP0 email, in the register of the most senior reader on the thread. Section order: (1) thanks and confirmation of what was received; (2) our understanding of the problem: the reframed statement in two or three sentences; (3) objectives and scope as we read them, with what we propose to add or exclude; (4) clarifying questions, numbered, each with why it matters; (5) proposed pathway and approach in one paragraph: the lens, the appraisal technique, the core deliverables; (6) proposed kickoff: date options, attendees, what we will bring; (7) what we need before kickoff. No attachment beyond a one-page summary if the RFP was long. The `communications` record is the system of record; a `deliverables` record is needed only if the Supervisor wants a QA trail.

## Capture after the alignment point

The PM's reply (or a call) closes AP0. `create` the inbound `communications` (`status: triaged`); for each answer `create` a `client_inputs` record (`kind: clarification`, `approval`, `directive` or `instruction`, `from`, `via → COM`, `content_verbatim`, `paraphrase`, `priority`, `disposition`), `update open_questions {status: answered, answer, answered_by → CIN, answered_at}` and `append` to `brief.clarifications`. If pathway or scope is challenged, revise the classification analysis as a new version and the brief, and record the change as a new decision with `supersedes`. When the reply confirms: `update brief {status: confirmed, version: 1}` (the server snapshots to `history/`); `update decisions` `scope` and `pathway` to `confirmed` with `made_by → STK`, `channel → COM`, `date`; set `engagement.pathway.confirmed_in` and `brief.confirmed_in`. `create` the `gate` decision (`gate: AP0`, `channel → COM`, `status: confirmed`); `request_approval` on it, action class `gate_transition`, risk `high`; on approval `set_gate AP0 passed` and `set_phase P1`. Record `actions` for anything promised and the kickoff as `meetings` `kind: kickoff`, `status: requested` or `scheduled`.

## Pitfalls

- Treating the RFP's problem statement as the problem. If as-reframed equals as-given, you have not thought.
- Classifying from the deliverable list ("they want an RIA, so regulatory") rather than from the decision at stake.
- Inventing client roles. If the ultimate decision maker is not named, raise an `open_questions` record and leave the field empty; the gate holds until it is answered.
- Spending the P0 research budget on the topic. The two runs are for the decision maker and the client entity.
- An AP0 email with more than eight questions, or with questions the RFP answers. Each costs credibility.
- Leaving `settings.meetings.disclosure_jurisdiction` at a default. Consent and recording rules differ by country; flag it to the Supervisor in the checkpoint.
- A confirmed decision without `made_by`, a `channel` and a `date` (rule 7). An email thread is a channel; a verbal nod is not.

## Checkpoint template

- `done`: documents imported (DOC ids); brief v0; N stakeholders; classification (ANA id); profiles started (PRF ids); AP0 email sent (COM id) or awaiting approval (APV id).
- `decided`: proposed primary and secondary pathway with a one-line rationale (DEC ids, `proposed`); scope reframing (DEC id).
- `learned`: the three things in the RFP that most change the engagement (the real decision, the real decision maker, the real deadline); any conflict between the RFP and the public record.
- `next`: triage the PM's reply; confirm scope and pathway; request the gate transition; start P1 framing.
- `blockers`: unanswered P1 clarifications (OQ ids); pending approval (APV id); jurisdiction setting to confirm.
