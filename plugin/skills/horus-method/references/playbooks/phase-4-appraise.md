# Phase 4: Appraise

Compare the shortlisted options rigorously with the pathway's appraisal method against one baseline and one assumptions register, and bring the client to a preferred option. Phase 4 ends at AP4. (The phase id in state is `P4`.)

**When Horus uses this:** `engagement.phase` is `P4` and gate `AP4` is not `passed`.

## Purpose and exit criteria

Produce appraisals a sceptical reviewer in a finance ministry or a regulatory oversight body would accept, and a comparison that makes the choice decidable. Gate AP4 (STATE_SCHEMA.md section 7) reports `ready` when: every `short_list` option has an `appraisals` record of the pack's `kind` at `reviewed` or `final`, all sharing the same `baseline.analysis` and the same `assumptions`; `sensitivity` is recorded for every quantified appraisal; the `option_comparison` analysis is `reviewed`; the appraisal deck and its method annex are released; and a `decisions` record of kind `preferred_option` is `confirmed`. Rules 14 and 20 apply: assumptions in a final appraisal must be `agreed` or flagged in `caveats`; the appraisal kind must be one the pack allows, with its method block complete. Exit is the AP4 `gate` decision, the `gate_transition` approval, `set_gate AP4 passed`, `set_phase P5`.

## Entry checks

`gates.AP3.status: passed`; `shortlist`, `guiding_principle`, `political_directive` and `criteria_weights` decisions `confirmed`; the `baseline` analysis at `reviewed`; shortlisted `options` each with `mechanism.key_assumptions` and `principles_alignment`; the reaction matrix at its post-workshop version; `engagement.currency`, `price_base_year` and `fiscal_year_start` set; `settings.research_budget.per_phase.P4` (default six runs) available; the `horus_appraisal` toolkit importable (the `analyst` checks on start).

## Step by step

1. **Fix the appraisal design.** From the pack's `Appraisal method`: `kind` (`ria`, `strategic_options`, `operating_model`, `delivery_intervention`, `business_case`, `service_concept`), `standards_applied`, `methodology` (`cba`, `cea`, `mca`, `scm`, `qualitative`, `mixed`), `proportionality_level`, `time_horizon`, the impact dimensions to assess, the distributional groups, the sensitivity method, the monitoring indicators expected. `create` a `decisions` record `kind: methodology`, `proposed_by: horus`, stating the design, and confirm it with the PM at the next check-in (`channel → COM`, `made_by → STK`) so the method cannot be disputed after the numbers appear. `append` the design to `approach.analytical_plan` as a revision (`status: revised`, version 2).
2. **Seed the assumptions register.** `create` `assumptions` for every parameter the models will share: `name`, `category`, `value`, `unit`, `low`, `high`, `distribution`, `basis` (`evidence`, `expert_judgement`, `client`, `convention`), `evidence → EVD` where the basis is evidence, `source_note`, `owner`, `status: proposed`. Include the discount rate and `update engagement.settings.default_discount_rate → ASM`. Send the client-sourced ones to the PM for confirmation; on reply `create` `client_inputs` `kind: data_provided` or `clarification` and `update assumptions {status: agreed, agreed_in → CIN}`. Unagreed assumptions stay `proposed` and must appear in `appraisals.caveats`.
3. **Finalise the baseline.** `update` the `baseline` analysis to `final` with the business-as-usual series the method needs over the time horizon. Every appraisal references this one record.
4. **Fan out the analysts.** One `analyst` per shortlisted option or package, in parallel, within the P4 budget. Brief: option ids, `baseline → ANA`, `kind` and `standards_applied`, `methodology`, `proportionality_level`, `time_horizon`, the dimensions, distributional groups, the assumption ids it may use, the sensitivity method and parameters, toolkit modules (`cba`, `scm`, `mca`, `montecarlo`, `distribution`, `trajectories`, `fivecase`, `report`), output formats (model, CSV, charts in the Horus theme). Each `create`s an `appraisals` record (`options`, `version: 1`, `kind`, `pathway`, `baseline`, `standards_applied`, `methodology`, `proportionality_level`, `time_horizon`, `discount_rate → ASM`, `impacts[]` with `estimate {central, low, high}` or `rating`, `affected_groups`, `assumptions`, `evidence`, `confidence`; `summary`; `distributional`; `feasibility {administrative, legal, political}`; `sensitivity`; `risks → RSK`; `monitoring`; the method block; `artefacts[]`; `caveats`; `status: draft`), `risks` records with `scope`, `likelihood`, `impact`, `mitigation`, `owner`, and any new `assumptions` at `proposed`. Every number carries a range; every input references an assumption or evidence id.
5. **Challenge each appraisal.** `get` the record with `expand`, and read the record, not the model. Check: every dimension the design named is present or marked not applicable; incidence is stated by who bears it; feasibility ratings agree with the reaction matrix and `internal_politics`; `caveats` list the unagreed assumptions; artefacts exist at their paths. Send back what fails with a written note. `update appraisals {status: reviewed, reviewed_by: principal}`; `update options {status: appraised, appraisal → APL}`.
6. **Compare across options.** Delegate one `analyst` or do it yourself: `create` `analyses` `kind: option_comparison` with `content {options, assessments → APL, criteria_weights → DEC, table, ranking, recommendation_candidates}`; run rank robustness (does the ranking survive the weight ranges and the sensitivity cases); check with `view appraisal_comparison`. Status `reviewed`.
7. **Reconcile with the politics.** For each candidate, update `options.principles_alignment` where the appraisal moved a score, `stakeholders.predicted_reactions` where incidence revealed a new loser, and `risks` of scope `political`. Raise unresolved conflicts between the numbers and the directives as `open_questions` `kind: political` for the readout.
8. **Propose the preferred option.** `create` `decisions` `kind: preferred_option`, `status: proposed`, `proposed_by: horus`, `statement`, `rationale`, `alternatives_considered` (why each other option falls away), `implications`. Write the readout storyline answer-first around it.
9. **Readout.** `create` `meetings` `kind: readout`, `conduct_mode: principal_co_led`, `decisions_sought` (preferred option or package; residual design parameters; assumptions to confirm). `create` `deliverables` `type: appraisal_deck`, `tier: T1`, and the method annex as `deliverables` `type: memo`, `tier: T2` (`appraisal_summary_table` on the investment pathway). `producer` renders both; the traceability audit must pass at first registration because every number traces to an assumption or evidence id; fresh `producer` review. Approvals: `tier0_tier1_release` (risk `medium`) for the deck, `tier2_release` (risk `low`) for the annex; `release_version`; pre-read under `client_pm_email`. Hold the readout per `alignment-point-protocol.md`, capture, gate, `checkpoint`.

## Pathway hooks

`Appraisal method` in `horus-policy/references/pathways/<id>.md` fixes the `kind`, the standards, the toolkit modules and the method block the server requires (rule 20): `regulatory` runs `ria` with `regulatory {sme_test, competition_check, compliance_cost_scm, enforcement_strategy, review_clause}` and the Standard Cost Model; `strategy` runs `strategic_options` with `strategic {criteria_weights, scenario_results, order_of_magnitude_cost}`; `institutional_operating_model` runs `operating_model {fit_to_mandate, capability_gap, transition_risk, legal_changes_required, cost_delta}`; `transformation_delivery` runs `delivery_intervention` with `delivery {trajectory, bottlenecks_addressed, effort, dependencies}`; `public_investment_business_case` runs `business_case` with `five_case` and `optimism_bias_uplift`; `programme_service_design` runs `service_concept` with `service {outcomes, take_up_estimate, cost_per_outcome, equity_assessment, pilot_design}`. The pack also sets `proportionality_level` by what is at stake and names the method reference under `horus-policy/references/appraisal/`.

## Client-facing artefact

Appraisal readout deck, Tier 1, with a Tier 2 method annex, both `DRAFT FOR DISCUSSION`. Deck order: (1) executive summary: the comparison in one exhibit and the emerging preferred option; (2) what was agreed at AP3: shortlist, principles, weights; (3) baseline and counterfactual; (4) method on one slide: standards, horizon, proportionality, what is monetised and what is rated; (5) appraisal comparison table with Harvey balls and ranges; (6) one slide per option: impacts by dimension and who bears them; (7) cost and benefit waterfall where monetised; (8) distributional incidence; (9) feasibility: administrative, legal, political; (10) risks; (11) sensitivity tornado and rank robustness; (12) the emerging preferred option and why the others fall away; (13) decisions sought; (14) next steps. Annex order: assumptions register with status, methods and standards, data sources, model descriptions, full sensitivity results, bibliography, traceability table.

## Capture after the alignment point

`decisions`: `update preferred_option` to `confirmed` with `made_by → STK[]`, `forum → MTG`, `date`, `rationale`, `alternatives_considered`, `affects → OPT`; `update options` to `preferred` and the others to `rejected` with the reason. Challenges to an assumption become `client_inputs` `kind: feedback` or `directive` with disposition, a new `assumptions` record (the old one `superseded`) and an `actions` re-run for the `analyst`. `open_questions` on data to firm up, with owner and due date. `actions` for re-runs and any extra sensitivity. `evidence` for stated positions on incidence. `risks` owners confirmed where the room assigned them. Then the `gate` decision, `gate_transition` approval (risk `high`), `set_gate AP4 passed`, `set_phase P5`, minutes email with the confirmed option restated in one sentence.

## Pitfalls

- Analysts seeding their own assumptions. Every parameter comes from the register; a new one is `proposed` with an owner and lands in `caveats`.
- Different baselines across options because each analyst "improved" it. The gate checks `baseline.analysis` equality; fix the baseline before the fan-out.
- Point estimates on slides. Ranges and confidence everywhere; a single number without a range fails the traceability audit's indicative rule.
- Monetising what cannot credibly be monetised to make the table look complete. Rate it, say why, and show it in the comparison.
- Political feasibility scored without reference to the reaction matrix. `feasibility.political.evidence` must cite interview evidence.
- A method annex produced after the deck. Build it first; the deck is a summary of it.
- Presenting the preferred option as settled. The deck says "emerging" until the `preferred_option` decision is `confirmed`.

## Checkpoint template

- `done`: appraisal design confirmed (DEC id); assumptions register (N agreed, M proposed); baseline final (ANA id); appraisals at `reviewed` (APL ids by option); comparison (ANA id); deck and annex (DEL ids, versions); readout held.
- `decided`: preferred option (DEC id, status); assumptions changed by the client (ASM ids).
- `learned`: which result the client disputed; the assumption the ranking is most sensitive to; where politics and arithmetic disagree.
- `next`: re-runs (ACT ids); recommendation storyline; implementation and communication planning; AP4 gate approval (APV id).
- `blockers`: unagreed assumptions in `caveats`; traceability audit failures; a preferred option not yet confirmed.
