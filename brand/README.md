# Horus brand

Edition 1, October 2026. The manual is `horus-brand-manual.html` (published as a private artifact for review); the assets are in `assets/`; the tokens in `assets/tokens.css`.

## What was decided, and from what

- **Mark: the Wedjat Lens.** A single-weight drawing of the Eye of Horus whose iris is a lens. The brow is the falcon's height (oversight), the almond is attention, the lens and focus point are the instrument, the teardrop and spiral are the falcon's cheek markings (the record kept, and the return). The focus point is the only element that changes colour: lapis while Horus is live.
- **Seal.** A round seal whose inner ring is the niche pattern of the serekh, the palace-facade frame that held the king's Horus name, the oldest royal seal. The solid mark sits at its centre. It goes only on work that has passed every Horus check: every claim traced, every option appraised, every stakeholder consulted, the adversarial review passed, every number reproducible.
- **Palette.** Obsidian, basalt and graphite grounds; platinum and silver text; royal gold for the mark, the seal and the one line that matters; lapis for the live state only. A platinum-paper light variant for documents.
- **Type.** Saira (variable width, engineered, DIN-like without copying SpaceX's D-DIN) for display and interface; Source Serif 4 for reading; IBM Plex Mono for data. Inter, Space Grotesk and Instrument Serif are named as faces the brand never uses.

## Benchmarks and what was taken from each

- *SpaceX roadshow deck*: black ground, white sans in capitals for display, thin rules, grey bars with one highlighted figure, the small signature glyph in the bottom corner of every page. Taken: restraint, the signed corner, chart language.
- *Palantir Foundry*: large grotesque, technical illustration, no decoration. Taken: findings as headings, engineering seriousness.
- *Canopy (Founders, Inc.)*: a single colour world, editorial prose, a visible form. Taken: one-colour discipline, prose over slogans, the form in the page.
- *Veeza and OpenClaw*: an icon that is the brand. Taken: the mark as a character with one live state, without a mascot.
- *YC Design Review, "How To Stand Out In A World Of AI Designs"*: say what it is and who it is for at the top; the form visible on the page; no scroll-jacking, blinking dots, glowing buttons, fake inputs, auto-advancing tabs or random bold words; animation only when it reinforces the point; a trust and security section for products that handle sensitive material; a long page reads as substance. All applied in `web/`.
